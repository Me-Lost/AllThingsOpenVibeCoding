Alien Tic-Tac-Toe

Win battles to claim squares. Three X in a line saves the day.

How to play

Get three X marks in a row, column or diagonal.

Choose an empty square to battle an alien for it. Each side starts with 2 health. The alien attacks; you pick the counter.
